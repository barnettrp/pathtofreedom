The Program

Eight weeks. Four stages. One path.

Every man's story is different, but the terrain of recovery follows a shape. This is the trail we walk together.

Weeks 1–2
Breaking Secrecy
Naming the struggle honestly and building your first accountability structure.
Weeks 3–4
Finding Root Causes
Exploring the wounds and beliefs driving the behavior, not just the behavior itself.
Weeks 5–6
Rebuilding Identity
Grounding who you are in grace, and replacing old patterns with new ones.
Weeks 7–8
Sustaining Freedom
A plan for the road ahead, with ongoing community built in.

The Grace Model
